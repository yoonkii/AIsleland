# VISUAL POLISH PLAYBOOK — AIsleland

## P0 — Do these first (highest impact, applies to our exact stack)

- **Hue-shifted tinted toon ramp (kill grayscale shadows)** — Rebuild the MeshToonMaterial `gradientMap` as a 6-texel RGB `DataTexture` instead of grayscale: band luminances [0.55, 0.55, 0.78, 0.78, 0.95, 1.0], but tint the dark band blue-violet (`rgb(96,88,158)`-family factor) and the top band warm cream (`rgb(255,244,224)`), with `texture.colorSpace = THREE.SRGBColorSpace`. Asymmetric bands: shadow occupies NdotL 0–0.45 (hard cut), mid 0.45–0.75, lit 0.75–1.0 (soft), plus a top 10% "kiss of light" (+8% value, warm). Alternative in `onBeforeCompile`: `outgoingLight = mix(shadowTint * diffuse, warmTint * diffuse, ramp)` — shadows are a COLOR at ~55% luminance, never darkened base. Effect: instant Ghibli richness; shadows read lavender, not dirty. Perf: free. (Lenses 1, 2, 4 all converge here — this is the consensus #1.)
- **Spherified normals on foliage** — At geometry-build time, for every canopy/bush/cloud vertex: `n = normalize(vertexPos - blobCenter)` (ellipsoid center for tall trees), `mix(originalNormal, spherified, 0.85)`, `normal.needsUpdate = true`. The whole canopy toon-shades as one plush ball with a single clean crescent shadow instead of per-poly noise. ~10 lines, zero runtime cost. THE Animal Crossing tree trick.
- **Vertex-baked fake AO + canopy darkening** — At build time write a `color` BufferAttribute (`vertexColors: true` multiplies into the ramp): (1) height gradient on grass/props: `mix(base*0.55, base, smoothstep(0,1,hNorm))`; (2) canopy interior: `col *= mix(0.72, 1.0, dist/canopyRadius)`; (3) blue-shift, don't gray-shift: multiply toward `vec3(0.62,0.60,0.82)`, floor 30% of albedo. Glues everything together; zero runtime cost.
- **Imperfection + hue-jitter pass on every placed prop** — Per instance: random Y rotation (full 2π), uniform scale 0.85–1.25 + independent Y stretch 0.95–1.1, lean 2–6° (≤7° hard cap) around a random horizontal axis, Poisson-disc placement (min-dist ≈ 1.5× canopy radius), 30% chance of 1–2 companions at 0.5–0.7 scale. Color: `instanceColor` with `offsetHSL(±0.03, ±0.06, ±0.05)`, hue slightly warmer at low altitude / cooler high. This is the entire "hand-crafted, not looped" illusion for near-zero code.
- **Time-of-day light script (keyframed color pairs, lerped in HSL/OKLCH)** — Replace physical blending with keyframes. DAY: key `#FFF4DC` int 1.6 / hemi sky `#BFE3FF` / hemi ground `#B9A6C9` (violet ground bounce = lavender daytime shadows). GOLDEN: `#FFC080` 1.8 / `#A8B8E8` / `#C97BA0`. DUSK: `#FF9E7A` 1.2 / `#7B6FB8` / `#5A4E8A`. NIGHT: `#9FB8E8` 0.5 / `#2E3560` / `#3A3160` + warm `#FFD9A0` point lights on props (warm-window-in-blue-night = the core cozy beat). Keep key:fill ≈ 3:1 by luminance. Fog + sky + hemi all driven from the same keyframe struct. Perf: free.
- **Miniature diorama camera + tilt-shift** — Long lens: FOV 25–35°, camera pulled back, pitched down 30–45°, locked composition (no free orbit) with mouse parallax only: `camera.position.lerp(base + vec3(mouse.x*0.6, mouse.y*0.3, 0), 0.01)` + shift the key light a few units with the cursor. Post: drei `<TiltShift2 blur={0.15} taper={0.5}/>` (cheaper and more "toy" than true DOF — prefer it over DepthOfField; if you want bokeh instead, `focalLength 0.02, bokehScale ≤3, height 480`, but pick ONE). Raise small-prop density 2–3× near the camera path. This is the "how is this a website?!" framing.

## P1 — Strong wins

- **Floating-island cliff strata** — Underside as 4–6 stacked shrinking rings (±5% vertex radius jitter) tapering to an inverted cone. Fragment: `band = floor((wPos.y + noise(wPos.xz*0.8)*0.4) / stratumH)` indexing `#b48a64 → #9a6b52 → #7d6a73 → #5a4a66` (hue drifts purple going down). Bottom 30% lerps toward fog color so the tip dissolves into air. Grass layer overhangs the first dirt stratum 3–5% with a bright rim vertex color (the AC "grass lip").
- **Height fog under the island** — `scene.fog = FogExp2(horizonColor, 0.015)` where horizonColor is sampled from the sky gradient's horizon band (same uniform drives both — mismatched fog/sky is the #1 giveaway). Patch `THREE.ShaderChunk.fog_fragment` once with `exp(-(wY - fogBaseY) * falloff)` so mist pools below the island and thins upward. Sells "floating" better than anything. Perf: chunk patch is free.
- **Warm back-rim on foliage (fake SSS), cool rim elsewhere** — Retune existing fresnel per material family: foliage rim `#D8F5A0` at 0.3–0.5, gated by back-light: `pow(1-dot(V,N),2.0) * max(dot(-L,V)*0.5+0.5, 0.0)`; props/characters keep pale sky rim `#CFE8FF`. Plus canopy vertical gradient: bottom ×0.75 hue −12°, top ×1.1 hue +8° (2 lines using local Y). Leaves read alive/translucent.
- **Depth-free stylized water + island-side foam ring** — Foam painted on the ISLAND shader, not water (Codrops abandoned the depth-RT approach — frame drops): world-Y stripe via two smoothsteps around `waterY + sin(t*speed)*amp`, foamDepth ≈ 0.05. Water: two perlin taps (caps thresholded at `0.6+0.01*sin(t*2)` + blotches), near/far color mix, vertex sine swell, voronoi sparkle `step(0.95, 1-F1)` that bloom catches. Single pass, no render targets.
- **ContactShadows as hero ground shadow** — `<ContactShadows opacity={0.5} scale={r*2.5} blur={2.5} far={4} resolution={512} frames={1} color="#3a2e5c"/>` (cool purple, never black; `frames={1}` for static geo — it's expensive live). Keep the 1024 directional map but drop `light.shadow.intensity` to ~0.4 under it. Blob shadows (radial CanvasTexture plane, `depthWrite:false`) under characters/pickups.
- **Instanced wind-swayed grass** — 5-vert blades, chunked InstancedMeshes (30–100k blades OK), unlit: `mix(groundColorAtInstance*0.85, tipColor, hFrac)` + root darkening `1-hFrac` — sampling the terrain color makes grass sit INTO the island. Wind in vertex shader, two octaves, height²-weighted: `pos.xz += hFrac² * amp * (sin(t*1.4+phase)*0.8 + sin(t*3.2+phase*1.7)*0.25)` with `phase = dot(instWorld.xz, vec2(0.5,0.31))` for traveling gusts. Apply the same injection to trees at amp = 5–8% of height. Motion is mandatory for shared GIFs.
- **Blobby clouds below AND above the island** — Merge 3 low-seg spheres (1.5/2.0/1.5 radius, 7×8 segs), jitter verts ±0.2/axis, **flatten underside** `v.y = max(v.y, -0.5)` (the load-bearing detail), toNonIndexed + computeVertexNormals. Bottom faces tinted `#dfe8f5`. 4–6 clusters, slow sine bob `sin(t*0.3+phase)*0.15`. Clouds BELOW the island sell altitude.
- **Icosphere rock/bush recipe** — Rocks: `IcosahedronGeometry(r,1)`, displace along normal `r*(0.10 + noise3(v*1.5)*0.15)`, squash y 0.6–0.8, flat-shade (toNonIndexed). Bushes: detail 2, 5–10% displacement, smooth/spherified normals. Add ±0.04r jitter to kill symmetry, then instance with hue jitter.

## P2 — Polish details

- **God-ray cones at golden hour** — 2–4 open ConeGeometry from the key light raking the cliff edge, AdditiveBlending, `depthWrite:false`, alpha = length falloff × `pow(1-dot(N,V),2)`, opacity 0.03–0.08, slow rotation. "Analytical volumetric" à la Lusion — no raymarching.
- **Dust motes** — drei `<Sparkles count={80} scale={12} size={2} speed={0.3} opacity={0.4} color="#fff6d8"/>` near camera; 50–150 max (more reads as snow). Bonus: spawn only inside god-ray cones.
- **Alpha-hashed fades** — `material.alphaHash = true; transparent = false` for grow-in/despawn animations and canopy intersections: dithered dissolve, no sort glitches, correct depth. Grain tamed by dpr 2 + SMAA.
- **Pitch-adaptive world curve** — Curve only along camera-forward (`y -= k * dz²`, log not bowl), and scale k with camera pitch (k at low angle ≈ 2× top-down) so the horizon always sits just behind the playfield (ACNH behavior).
- **Paper-grain finishing Effect** — Custom pmndrs `Effect`: procedural hash-noise paper grain at 3–5% opacity (SOFT_LIGHT), uv wobble `uv += (noise(uv*40 + t*0.1)-0.5)*0.0015`, vignette tinted toward warm paper, not black. Merges into the existing effect shader — near-free. The current "hand-made" differentiator (Susurrus).
- **Palette audit** — 4–5 anchor families only: grass green ~60% of pixels, cliff/dirt ~25%, warm accents ≤10% (only accents may exceed 75% sat; environment clamped 35–60% sat). Grass/tree/cliff hues within ±25° of each other, separated by VALUE (~+0.12 V steps). No pure white/black anywhere. Screenshot every biome × time-of-day as a grid and check cohesion.
- **Chunky proportions** — Trunks 2–3× realistic thickness, canopy 60–70% of tree height, nothing thinner than 0.05× object height, bevel every hard edge (RoundedBoxGeometry) so toon bands catch on edges.
- **Skip outlines** — Postprocess edge detection is the wrong register for pastel-soft; the fresnel rim already separates silhouettes. If a hero prop needs definition: inverted hull, BackSide, width 0.008–0.015 world units, colored a darker-saturated version of the object's own hue — never black, never global.
- **Loader→reveal choreography** — Preload via `useProgress`, fullscreen ScreenQuad wipe shader (`uProgress`), then one GSAP dolly-in (power2.inOut, ~2.5s) from high/far to the hero framing as clouds part. One motion principle reused everywhere. Production value is judged in the first 3 seconds.

## Global grade pass (exact settings, pastel cozy)

- **Tone mapping: `THREE.NoToneMapping`.** Conflict resolution: Lens 3's filmic instincts lose to Lens 2's evidence — ACESFilmic desaturates and hue-skews hand-picked pastels; our colors are authored, not HDR. If night-scene bloom blows out, switch to a final `<ToneMapping mode={ToneMappingMode.AGX}/>` effect and compensate with saturation +0.15 — but try No first.
- **Renderer**: `outputColorSpace: SRGBColorSpace` (leave default; composer does the final encode — no manual pow(1/2.2) anywhere), `dpr={[1, 2]}`, shadow map stays 1024 PCFSoft at `shadow.intensity ≈ 0.4` under ContactShadows. Run all hand-authored GLSL vec3 color literals through `convertSRGBToLinear`.
- **Fog**: `FogExp2(horizonColor, 0.015)` + height-falloff patch (base at water level, falloff ~0.35); horizonColor keyframed with the light script.
- **Post chain** (`<EffectComposer frameBufferType={THREE.HalfFloatType}>`, effects auto-merge — 2 real passes total):
  1. `<Bloom mipmapBlur luminanceThreshold={0.85} intensity={0.6} radius={0.7}/>` (night: threshold 0.7, intensity 1.2; optional second wide bloom radius 1.1 intensity 0.3 for haze)
  2. `<TiltShift2 blur={0.15} taper={0.5}/>`
  3. Lift-gamma-gain grade (replaces bare HueSaturation): `pow(max(vec3(0), c*(1+gain-lift)+lift), max(vec3(0), 1-gamma))` with lift `vec3(0.03,0.02,0.05)` (lavender shadows), gain `vec3(1.02,1.0,0.95)` (cream highlights), gamma `vec3(0.05)`; keep HueSaturation saturation +0.10..0.15; animate lift/gain with time-of-day
  4. `<ChromaticAberration offset={[0.002,0.002]} radialModulation modulationOffset={0.7}/>` (outer 30% of frame only)
  5. Paper-grain Effect (3–5%, SOFT_LIGHT) + `<Noise opacity={0.02}/>` (kills gradient banding)
  6. `<Vignette offset={0.3} darkness={0.5}/>` tinted warm
  7. `<SMAA/>` (preset ULTRA)
- **Perf gate**: quality tiers — mobile drops TiltShift, CA, and halves grass count; `frames={1}` on ContactShadows/Environment; everything else above is analytic/single-pass.
